SafeSteps Terms

SafeSteps is intended for transparent family safety use by parents or guardians. It must not be used for hidden or unauthorized tracking.

Permissions

The child device shows an ongoing notification while location sharing is active and provides controls to stop sharing.

Subscriptions

Paid plans are managed through Google Play Billing. Subscription terms, trials, and renewals are displayed by Google Play at purchase time.

Availability

Location accuracy and update frequency depend on device settings, battery state, operating system limits, and network availability.
